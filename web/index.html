Qualia Anatomy

Open the part demo. Default is the working heart master. Use the Lane control to compare working CAD and the published package. The demo is QualiaPortal-only: it loads .10d (SRD1/UV01) and the sibling .emf (Q42EMF64 field values). A 2D PNG is a labeled degraded fallback when GPU is unavailable, not the official painter.

Research lives in master/. Shipped files live in distribution/. The .hmc is the container around those files.
